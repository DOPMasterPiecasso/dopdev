10. Direction update (revisi desain)
Arah desain baru yang dipilih:
    • Sederhana, premium, dan sangat fokus ke conversion.
    • Gunakan logo DOPDEV yang sudah ada di website ini.
    • Hindari footer dan header global agar terasa seperti landing page campaign murni.
    • Prioritaskan trust-building, jelasnya offer, dan CTA yang langsung ke WhatsApp.
    • Warna utama tetap netral dengan aksen hijau lembut agar terasa bersih, professional, dan mudah dibaca.
    • Layout dibuat lebih "power full" untuk pemilik bisnis: jelas siapa target, kenapa butuh website, apa manfaatnya, dan apa yang harus dilakukan selanjutnya.


    promp design :

    Design and build a single-page landing page (HTML + CSS + minimal vanilla JS, no frameworks) for Dopdev, a web design studio selling a fixed-price landing page to home service businesses in Singapore (aircon, cleaning, electrical, plumbing, renovation). The ONE goal of the page: get the visitor to tap a WhatsApp button to request a free homepage mockup.

AUDIENCE
Business owners aged 30-60. Practical, time-poor, skeptical of agencies, mostly on mobile. They want to feel "this is clear, honest and professional", not "this is trendy". Design for trust and readability first, delight second.

VISUAL DIRECTION
- Predominantly white. Background #FFFFFF with very light warm-gray sections (#F7F7F5) for separation. Text in near-black ink (#111827). One single accent color used only for actions and key numbers: deep green #16A34A (hover #15803D). No gradients, no dark mode, no stock-photo clutter.
- Style: modern editorial minimalism. Lots of whitespace, big confident typography, thin 1px borders (#E5E7EB), soft large-radius cards (16-20px) with a very subtle shadow.
- Typography: one clean sans-serif family (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans). Headlines 48-72px desktop / 34-42px mobile, weight 700-800, tight tracking (-0.02em), line-height 1.1. Body 18-20px, line-height 1.65, color #374151. Never use text smaller than 15px. Never use light-gray text on white; keep contrast WCAG AA or better.
- Buttons: large (min height 56px), pill or 12px radius, solid green with dark text or white text with strong contrast, clear label. Tap targets at least 48px.

ANIMATION (the "wow", but calm and professional)
The animation comes from TEXT and CARDS only. No parallax, no spinning, no flashy effects.
1. Hero headline: line-by-line mask reveal on load (each line slides up from behind an overflow-hidden wrapper, 80ms stagger). The words "Phone Ringing." get an animated green underline that draws in left to right after the reveal.
2. Section headlines: fade + rise 24px on scroll into view, using IntersectionObserver, once only.
3. Cards: staggered entrance (120ms between cards), then on hover lift 6px with a slightly stronger shadow and the border turning accent green. On touch devices, use a subtle press (scale 0.98) instead of hover.
4. Price card: the price counts up from 0 to 690 once when visible. A small "5 spots only" badge has a soft pulsing dot.
5. Comparison table: rows slide in one by one; the Dopdev column is highlighted in a pale green tint.
6. FAQ: smooth height animation on open/close, plus rotating "+" icon.
7. Sticky bottom WhatsApp bar on mobile that appears after scrolling past the hero (slides up).
8. Timing: 400-700ms, easing cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling all motion.

1. Slim top bar: logo "DOPDEV" + "WhatsApp Us" button.
2. Hero: badge "Launch offer: S$690 (usual S$990) · Only 5 spots this month"; H1 "A Website That Gets Your Phone Ringing. Ready in 7 Days."; subhead "Landing pages for Singapore home service businesses: aircon, cleaning, electrical, plumbing and renovation. Fixed price. WhatsApp enquiries built in. Pay the deposit only after you approve the design."; button "Get Your Free Homepage Mockup on WhatsApp"; small text "Send your business name and we'll show you a draft before you pay anything."; three trust ticks (Fixed price / Mobile-first / Hosting + domain setup in year 1).
3. Problem: H2 "When someone searches 'aircon servicing near me,' you have about 5 seconds." Three cards: Looks trustworthy / Loads fast on a phone / Is easy to contact. Use simple line icons, not emoji.
4. Offer: one large centered card. "The Enquiry-Ready Landing Page", S$690 with S$990 struck through, checklist of 9 inclusions, "Not included" note, button "Claim One of the 5 Spots".
5. Value: H2 "One new job can pay for the whole website." Comparison table: DIY vs Typical agency vs Dopdev (cost, ready in, built for enquiries).
6. Portfolio: H2 "See what yours could look like." Three cards (Contractor, Cleaning Service, Electrician), each with a browser-frame mockup placeholder area and a "View live demo" link.
7. How it works: 4 numbered steps in a horizontal row (stacked on mobile), with a thin connecting line that draws as you scroll.
8. Promise: a calm bordered box: "You see the design before you pay. If you don't like the direction, you walk away and owe nothing."
9. FAQ: 5 accordion items.
10. Final CTA: "Only 5 spots at S$690 this month." + WhatsApp button.
11. Footer with PDPA consent line.

TECHNICAL RULES
- Mobile-first, fully responsive, tested at 360px width.
- Semantic HTML, proper heading order, alt text, visible focus states.
- All WhatsApp buttons link to https://wa.me/6282125189383 with a prefilled message.
- Page weight under 150 KB, no external libraries, no web fonts beyond one family.
- Do not invent testimonials, client logos, ratings or statistics.

Return one complete index.html with embedded CSS and JS, commented by section, ready to deploy.
