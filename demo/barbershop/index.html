FAQ

Questions we get from owners

We already take bookings on Instagram DMs — why change?

DMs work until you're mid-haircut. Messages get buried, hours pass, and that client already booked someone else. A booking system answers instantly, every time — including the 40% of enquiries that arrive after closing.

Will my barbers actually use it?

Yes — it's built around how your shop already works: service menu, barber rotation, branch layout. Front-desk training is included at go-live, and the dashboard needs no manual or spreadsheets.

Do I have to switch everything at once?

No. Many shops start with a revamp plus one or two modules — booking and WhatsApp reminders first, deposits and CRM later. The system is built to extend without rebuilding.

What about Booksy or Timely?

They're fine to start — until you grow: monthly rent forever, per-location surcharges, and your client data locked in their cloud. A DOP system is a one-time build you own, shaped around your rules. See the comparison with real projects →

How fast until it's live?

Four weeks from kick-off for typical barbershop scopes: 3-day audit, 3 weeks build, go-live with training in week 4. The exact timeline is confirmed before we start.
